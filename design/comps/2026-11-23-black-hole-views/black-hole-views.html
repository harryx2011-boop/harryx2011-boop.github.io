ABent disk

The camera sits 10 degrees above the disk. Gravity bends light from the far side up and over the shadow, so the disk shows twice. One side is brighter because that gas moves toward you. Strongest silhouette, most to look at.

BRing

The camera looks down at 75 degrees. The disk becomes rings around a black circle, with a thin bright ring of trapped light at its edge. Calm and symmetric. Words can sit under it or inside it.

CHorizon

The camera skims the disk at 3 degrees, close in. The frame crops the hole and the disk becomes one bright line with an arc of bent light above it. Shows less of the hole and leaves the most empty space for words.
