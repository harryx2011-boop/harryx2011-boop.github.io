Three layouts for the Horizon hero

Each layout is shown as your first screen on a desktop (1440 px wide) and on a phone (390 px wide). The black hole is live in every frame. The structure of each one comes from a real site in the reference study; the sizes named below were measured there. Pick a layout, and use the switch to pick the colour.

Colour of the black hole, in every frame

1Title card

The role is the headline. Your name is the credit line under it, like the opening card of a film.

Structure from adrienquechon.com: a capital serif headline at 108 px with 0.85 line height, a small mono credit row under it, corner marks, mono links at the top right. On a phone the headline drops to 58 px.

2Introduction

Your own sentence is the hero. The disk cuts the screen on a diagonal and the words sit in the quiet corner it leaves.

Structure from windowtomars.com: a light headline at 44.6 px in a left column, the graphic on the right, and on a phone the graphic moves above the words. The 96 px bar with a pill of links is from carterogunsola.com.

3Baseline

The bright line of the disk is the line your name is written on. Almost nothing else is on the screen.

Structure from nahiyan.codes: four tabs at the bottom, 11 px mono with wide spacing, each 44 px tall so a thumb can hit it. The three-part top bar at 11.2 px is from zenobasi.com.

What the study could not measure

The ten reference sites gave real numbers for type, bars and colours. None of them gave a timing for a pinned scroll scene, because the motion capture tool failed twice. The scroll pacing in the build therefore starts from my own estimate and gets tuned by eye.
